        make this your home page

        so your classes are the first thing you see every time you open chrome.

        1. Hit copy link up top.
        2. In Chrome, click ⋮ (top right) → Settings.
        3. Click On startup → Open a specific page or set of pages → Add a new page → paste → Add.
        4. Then click Appearance → turn on Show home button → click the box under it and paste the link there too. Now the 🏠 button brings you back here.

        iPhone Chrome doesn't have a home page setting, so turn it into an app instead:

        1. Tap open home link below.
        2. Tap the share button (□↑). In Chrome it's in the address bar, in Safari it's at the bottom.
        3. Tap Add to Home Screen → Add.
        4. Open it from the new icon on your home screen ✿
